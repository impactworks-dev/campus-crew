# Campus Crew launch plan (summary)

Full interactive plan lives in ClickUp. This is the short version.

## Crew
- 1 Campus Lead ($100/mo stipend + 5% override on crew sales)
- 8 ambassadors, 2 specialists per agent. Everyone sells all four.

## Pay
- 30% of each paying user's first 90 days (10% on the $199 Furnished Room bundle)
- $5 per completed Move-Out pickup (3+ items)
- XP for free signups and activations; monthly prizes $50 / $30 / $20

## Sequence (UNCW 2026-27 calendar)
| Agent | Window | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Syllabus Escape Room | Nov 30 to Dec 10 | Finals Dec 4 to 10, Reading Day Dec 3 |
| Side Quest | Dec 10 teaser, Jan 8 launch | Halls open Jan 8, classes Jan 11 |
| The Forgotten Refund | Jan 11 to Feb 5 | Holiday free trials start charging |
| Move-Out Flip | Mar 8 to May 7, then Jun to Aug | Halls close May 7, 3 PM |

## Guardrails
- Every ambassador post discloses ("I'm an ambassador" / #ambassador)
- No UNCW logos or official claims
- Campus posting rules first; ask Housing & Residence Life before hall flyers
- Escape Room is never pitched as homework help
- Ambassadors never see user data
- Payouts monthly after a 14-day refund window

## Budget
About $2,000 through May (stipend, prizes, stickers, two launch moments, texting). Move-Out truck + storage (est. $1,000 to $1,400) locked until 40 pickups are booked.
