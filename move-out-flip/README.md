# UNCW Move-Out Flip

Text agent that books free pickups of furniture students leave at move-out, stores it over summer, resells it to incoming students in August, and pays sellers half. Not affiliated with UNCW.

- Seller sign-ups Mar 8 to Apr 29, 2027 · pickups Apr 30 to May 7 (halls close May 7, 3 PM) · First Dibs sales Jun to Aug · move-in delivery mid-Aug.
- Pricing (concept): Sellers free, keep 50% · $9 First Dibs Pass · $199 Furnished Room bundle.
- Storage spend stays locked until 40 pickups are booked.
- Signature: flip calculator (curb $0 vs August value, your cut).

Files: `index.html` + `assets/` (floater.webp, still.webp, grain.png).
